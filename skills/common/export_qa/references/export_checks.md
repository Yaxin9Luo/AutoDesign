# Export checks

Plan editable exports: native text/tables, named layers, stable ids, and source-grounded images. Poster outputs remain PSD/SVG/HTML; landing is semantic self-contained HTML; deck exports are editable `deck.html` plus `deck.pdf`; video keeps MP4 and an editable scene stack when available. Record the font, visual profile, imagery source, and explicit tradeoffs in composition notes.

Treat blockers, P0 lint, `frame_layout_*`, `html_artifact_contract_*`, sanitizer, provenance, closing-slide, and source-visual findings as one queue. A clean preview is insufficient when editability, provenance, or source evidence is missing.

Repair blockers first. Fix slots and groups before child spacing; prefer local editable operations. Preserve source figures and brand assets by resizing, cropping, annotating, or simplifying. Never replace an editable frame with a full-page raster to hide a contract failure.
