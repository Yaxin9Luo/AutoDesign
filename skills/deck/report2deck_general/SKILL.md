# Report2Deck

Use for non-academic report-backed decks. Keep report evidence distinct from generated mood imagery.

## Stage: enhance

Classify report and audience, then keep the narrative provisional until ingest confirms the source structure, evidence, and decision goal.

## Stage: plan

Ingest first; plan an evidence-led slide arc with source slots, grouped blocks, and stable metadata. Read `report_scenario_map`.

## Stage: critique

Check source references, readable charts/tables, slide-native narrative, scenario coverage, and any misleading decorative imagery.

## Stage: repair

Repair slots and evidence before polish; switch to paper provenance constraints when the source is an academic paper. Read `report_scenario_map`.
