# Campaign and event posters

Campaign, event, social, and product posters need one first-read hook, one dominant visual or graphic field, three to five supporting facts, and a clear footer. Choose a single archetype rather than stacking generic cards.

Keep the final PSD/SVG/HTML editable. Generated imagery may carry atmosphere but not factual evidence. Use hierarchy, scale, composition, and limited structural accents instead of filler metrics, emoji iconography, generic gradients, repeated cards, or placeholder copy. Repair the composition before changing cosmetic color details.
