# Region geometry

Identify the identity header first, then assign each top-level body section or callout to exactly one macro region. Use `column`, `footer_band`, `side_callout`, `hero_region`, `stacked_region`, or `full_width_band`. Partition by continuous primary reading tracks; promote a rail only when it is an independent top-level zone. Keep detached bottom question or Terminus content as its own region.

Region boxes follow observed content, exclude gutters/chrome, stay inside canvas, and never overlap. Preserve spans, proportions, alignment, gaps, and reading order. Group vertically aligned panels into a stacked region when a poster otherwise exceeds six regions; do not count internal figure/table frames as regions.
