# Paper Poster Revision

Use only to repair an existing academic paper poster. Keep revisions paper-grounded, local, editable, and measured.

## Stage: repair

Read the poster, requested changes, and supplied source evidence before edits. Classify the finding as content, layout, asset correctness, typography, or export. Read `layout_repair`; use `manual_asset_policy` for figures, tables, and identity assets; use `export` for final output checks. Do not perform automatic logo lookup. Only when the user explicitly requests a logo in this revision may you retrieve an exact official asset under the manual asset policy.
